# Daily use

Once your home and appliances are set up, PowerPlan runs on its own. This page covers what you might change from day to day.

<a name="control"></a>
## Trial mode and automatic control

**Automatic control** on your home switches PowerPlan between steering your appliances and only showing what it would do. Each appliance also has its own **Control**: **Automatic**, **Run now**, **Don't control**, **Trial mode**, or **Controlled by something else** when another automation or integration steers it.

When you switch **Automatic control** off, PowerPlan hands every appliance back as it found it.

<a name="presence"></a>
## Presence

**Presence** on your home is **Automatic** when PowerPlan follows the people you picked in setup. Set it to **Away** or **Vacation** yourself, or from an automation, to lower comfort while nobody is home. Each heating appliance has **Save when nobody is home** to take part or not.

<a name="run_now"></a>
## Run now

**Run now** on an appliance runs it at full power for a while, whatever the price, for example when you need the car charged now. It ends by itself after **Run now for at most**. It still respects your fuse.

<a name="pause"></a>
## Pausing an appliance

Set its **Control** to **Don't control** to take it out of PowerPlan's hands, or to **Trial mode** to see what PowerPlan would do with it. PowerPlan never pauses an appliance you set to **Don't control**.

<a name="targets"></a>
## Targets and comfort

Change the home's **Capacity step target** and **Strictness** at any time; the next plan uses them. For each appliance, change **Comfort temperature**, **Never colder than**, **Ready by** or **Charge to** on its device.

<a name="notifications"></a>
## Notifications

PowerPlan tells you about a coming peak, comfort it could not keep, and a device that stopped responding. Choose which, and where, with **Reconfigure** on the home. Everything else is an entity you can put on a dashboard or use in an automation.
